# TheLook E-Commerce Analytics & Forecasting Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

TheLook E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project built in Microsoft Excel using Power Pivot, DAX, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive dashboard navigation.

The project transforms e-commerce transaction data into interactive dashboards that help stakeholders understand business performance, operational efficiency, product and customer performance, and future sales trends.

The dashboard is organized into three main sections:

- **Executive Dashboard** – high-level business performance and KPIs
- **Operations Dashboard** – inventory, products, categories, brands, and distribution centers
- **Forecasting Dashboard** – future order and revenue trends

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Monitor overall e-commerce performance through key KPIs
- Analyze revenue and profit trends over time
- Understand order status and returned orders
- Identify the best-performing products, categories, and brands
- Evaluate distribution center performance
- Analyze inventory distribution across categories and brands
- Identify the top customers by revenue
- Forecast future monthly orders and revenue
- Present complex business data through clear and interactive dashboards

## 📈 Dashboard Structure

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through:

- Total Revenue
- Profit
- Completed Orders
- Returned Orders
- Total Orders
- Customers
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Order Status Distribution
- Top 10 Categories by Revenue
- Top 3 Products by Revenue
- Top 5 Brands by Revenue
- Top Distribution Centers by Revenue
- Top 10 Customers by Revenue

### 2. Operations Dashboard

Focuses on inventory and operational performance.

**Main KPIs:**
- Inventory Items
- Products
- Distribution Centers
- Categories
- Brands

**Main visuals:**
- Inventory Items by Distribution Center
- Inventory Items by Category
- Inventory Items by Brand

### 3. Forecasting Dashboard

Provides a forward-looking view of the business through:

- Monthly Orders Forecast
- Monthly Revenue Forecast
- Historical vs. Forecasted Performance
